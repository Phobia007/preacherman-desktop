# Behaviors

- Featured rail: wheel, pointer drag, and touch drive the original inertial WebGL strip.
- Card hover: original WebGL deformation and shade response.
- Profile: click toggles the source profile scene; no replacement UI.
- Featured / Full: source navigation and motion are retained inside the iframe.
- Entry: a semantic-theme white/contrast line traverses left to right, the mask opens a narrow band, briefly contracts like a pulse, then completes the vertical reveal.
- Reduced motion: the line and mask settle immediately without repeated pulsing.
- Light and dark: source-authored media is unchanged; the wrapper, focus state, loading surface, and reveal line use `--demo-theme-*` tokens.
